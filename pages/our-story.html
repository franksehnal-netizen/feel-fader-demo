Our story

We are Ivan and František – friends brought together by music, which we write, mix and master ourselves. At some point in our work together we realised that even with foam “pyramids” on the walls, our room still just didn't sound right. We wanted to find out where the problem was, so we started looking for solutions and building our first prototypes.

Even the first version had a huge wow effect on us, and we were so excited that we knew this was a direction worth exploring much more deeply. So here we are: we replaced the ordinary materials we started with by ones that do the job best, and we make our products with care, precise planning and a commitment to help wherever a single clap makes you want to cover your ears.

From the room to the instrument

Working on our own music, we kept running into the same thing: orchestral libraries come alive through two continuous moves – dynamics and expression – and doing them with a mouse or a single small fader never felt like playing. So we built the controller we wanted on our own desk.

Feel Fader puts two long faders under your hands, with a roller for switching articulations and a button for banks and shortcuts. We designed the hardware, the firmware and the web configurator ourselves, and refined them with feedback from working composers. The whole setup lives on the controller, so it plays the same in every studio you plug it into.

Passion drives everything we do

Our love for sound and handcraft goes into every product we make. We don't just sell products – we want you to unlock the full potential of your space and your music, whether it is a recording studio, a living room or an office.
