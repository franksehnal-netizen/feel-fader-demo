CC Remapper for Ableton Live

Max for Live MIDI effect · by Acoustic Empire

CC REMAPPER (for Feel Fader) is a Max for Live device made by Acoustic Empire specifically for Feel Fader. The roller's Relative CC mode sends only up/down steps – it does not know which articulation comes next. Placed on a track in Ableton Live, the device turns those steps into keyswitch notes, so you can switch articulations with the roller while you ride the faders.

What it does

  • Roller – each Relative CC step (1 = up, 127 = down) moves one note up or down inside the keyswitch range you set and plays that keyswitch. At the ends of the range it stops instead of wrapping around.
  • Left and right fader – remap the fader's incoming CC to the CC your instrument expects.

Requirements

  • Ableton Live with Max for Live.
  • Feel Fader with firmware 1.3.0 or newer.

Set up Feel Fader

  1. In the Feel Fader app, open the bank you use for the track.
  2. Set the roller to Relative CC and pick its MIDI channel and CC (default CC 32).
  3. Send the configuration to the device.

Install in Live

  1. Copy CC REMAPPER (for Feel Fader).amxd to User Library/Presets/MIDI Effects/Max MIDI Effect/. Download link coming soon.
  2. Drag the device onto the MIDI track, in front of your instrument.
  3. Set the roller's CC in to the CC you chose in the app.
