In short

The Feel Fader configurator runs entirely in your browser. It has no user accounts, sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools and loads nothing from third-party services. Your settings are never sent to us.

What stays in your browser

To keep your work between visits, the app saves the following in your browser's local storage on your computer:

  • a backup copy of your controller configuration (banks, mappings, names),
  • library setups you have saved and the ones you used recently,
  • display preferences (light/dark mode, note naming) and which introductory hints you have already seen,
  • technical identifiers of the last connected controller (USB product ID, serial number and a configuration checksum), used to reconnect and to detect changes made elsewhere.

This data never leaves your computer. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What is stored on your controller

The configuration you send, including an optional owner name, is stored in the memory of your Feel Fader. We have no access to it.

Network requests

The app and firmware update files are downloaded from the same server that serves this page. When delivering them, the hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. – confirm final hosting) processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs. We do not receive or use this data.

When you contact us

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message to answer you and keep the correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

The support snapshot under Device › MIDI diagnostics is copied only to your clipboard. It reaches us only if you choose to send it. It contains technical data about your controller and configuration (firmware version, active bank mapping, last received MIDI event, keyboard (HID) state and configuration checksum).
