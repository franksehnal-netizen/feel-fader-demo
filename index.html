    Getting started

    Connect your Feel Fader via USB and click Connect & load. The configurator reads the device config; after editing, click Send to device to apply your changes. Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ + S sends, Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes the last change.

    Faders

    Each fader sends a MIDI CC on a channel you choose (Expression / Dynamics by default). The on-screen faders mirror the hardware – they are display only and cannot be dragged.

    Banks

    Bank names and icons are saved in this browser and – with firmware 1.1.0+ – also on the device, so they follow it between computers. The flat pill is the bank you're editing; a small green dot marks the bank currently active on the hardware. Library setups show a review before changing the bank; built-in setups are starting points and should be checked against the exact library patch.

    Roller modes

    Articulation – the roller steps through articulations (UACC). Keyswitch – the roller sends note keyswitches selected on a piano keyboard (see below). Navigation – the roller sends a keyboard key per detent (e.g. arrows) to navigate your DAW; requires Keyboard (HID). Relative CC – the roller sends a relative step (value 1 = up, 127 = down) on the configured CC/channel, for external MIDI processing (e.g. a per-track keyswitch mapper).

    Keyswitch mode

    In Keyswitch mode the roller sends MIDI note keyswitches on the configured channel. Click one piano key or drag across a range; rotating the roller steps through the selected notes. Reorder long sequences by dragging rows or with Alt + arrow keys.

    Button macro (long-press)

    A long-press (≥ 0.5 s) of the device button sends a defined key combo – e.g. play/stop or a DAW shortcut. Requires Keyboard (HID). A short press still cycles banks. You can set the key combo while Keyboard is off; it is sent once Keyboard is on.

    Keyboard (HID)

    Turn Keyboard on to let the device send keystrokes – required for the Navigation roller mode and the button long-press macro. The switch sits at the top of the Button section and of Navigation; it is one setting for the whole device. Changing it needs Feel Fader connected, in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

    MIDI diagnostics

    Open MIDI diagnostics under Device to inspect the active bank mapping, last received MIDI event, firmware, HID state and config hash, or copy a support snapshot.

    Switching banks

    On the hardware, the button cycles through your banks. You can define up to 8 banks.
